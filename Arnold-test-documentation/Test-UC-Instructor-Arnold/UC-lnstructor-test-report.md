## 2. What I Observed

* Different question types can be managed effectively.
* The preview feature helps instructors review survey content before publishing.

Overall, the functionality supports the main survey management requirements.

## 3. What the Use Case Says

According to the Manage Survey use case, the system should allow instructors to:

* Create a survey by defining its title, description, and dates.
* Add and manage survey questions.
* Edit an existing survey.
* Delete a survey when it is no longer needed.
* Preview a survey before making it available to participants.

The use case also specifies that the system should handle special situations such as:

* Missing survey titles during creation.
* Errors when saving changes.
* Problems encountered during survey preview.

In addition, administrators should be able to review surveys, view survey results, and disable surveys when necessary.

# Areas for Improvement

As part of a continuous improvement approach, integrating search and filtering capabilities could make survey management simpler and more efficient.

Implementing an automatic save feature for responses could also enhance the user experience by helping preserve data entered before final submission.


# Conclusion

Overall, the Manage Survey functionality meets its primary objectives and aligns with the main requirements described in the use case. The core features, including survey creation, question management, survey editing, and response review, are available and functional.

The observations made during testing highlight opportunities for further enhancement, particularly in terms of usability and data management. Addressing these areas could contribute to an even more efficient and user-friendly survey management experience.

In general, the feature provides a solid foundation for creating and managing surveys within the platform.
