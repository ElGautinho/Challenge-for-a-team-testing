# TEST NOTES — Instructor Survey Management Workflow

---

## What I am testing

I am testing:

> Instructor survey management workflow and cross-role survey lifecycle behavior

The objective is to verify:

* survey creation and publication
* question management
* student response workflow
* instructor response monitoring
* data consistency between Instructor and Student roles
* survey lifecycle behavior after edits, deletion, and restoration

The testing covers:

* UC-Manage Survey
* UC-Manage Question Type
* UC-Provide Feedback
* UC-Review Past Feedback

---

## What I did (steps)

1. Logged in as Instructor
2. Created and published surveys
3. Added multiple questions to surveys:

   * text questions
   * multiple choice questions
   * true/false questions
4. Linked questions to surveys
5. Logged in with multiple Student accounts
6. Submitted survey responses
7. Verified responses in Instructor “Survey Responses” view
8. Modified survey titles and question content
9. Deleted questions and surveys
10. Verified Student completed history after deletion
11. Restored deleted surveys and questions
12. Tested preview functionality
13. Tested refresh behavior before submission

---

## What I noticed

* Survey creation and publication work correctly
* Questions can be created and linked successfully
* Multiple question types behave correctly
* Students can access and complete surveys
* Responses are stored and visible to the Instructor
* Role separation works correctly between Student and Instructor accounts
* Surveys/questions can be restored after deletion
* Empty surveys and empty questions can still be published
* Unsaved student responses are lost after refresh
* Deleted questions disappear from Student history but remain in Instructor response history
* Deleted surveys disappear for Instructor but remain in Student completed history
* Instructor preview mode does not simulate Student experience

---

## What should have happened

Based on the documented use cases:

* Mandatory fields should be validated before publishing surveys/questions
* Student progress should persist before submission
* Historical survey/question behavior should remain consistent across roles
* Deleted entities should follow a unified visibility policy
* Instructor preview should provide a student-like rendering of surveys

Yes

## Summary of issues

### 1. Missing validation on mandatory fields

**Observed behavior:**

* Surveys without titles can be published
* Questions without content can be saved and published

**Impact:**
Creates structurally invalid surveys and inconsistent data states.

**Type:**
Validation / Data Integrity

**Severity:**
High 🔴

---

### 2. Missing persistence for incomplete student responses

**Observed behavior:**

* Refreshing the page removes all unsaved responses
* No autosave or recovery mechanism exists

**Impact:**
Causes complete loss of student progress during survey completion.

**Type:**
Workflow / Data Persistence

**Severity:**
High 🔴

---

### 3. Inconsistent historical question behavior after deletion

**Observed behavior:**

* Deleted questions disappear from Student completed history
* Instructor response history still contains associated responses

**Impact:**
Creates different historical states between Student and Instructor views.

**Type:**
Data Consistency / Historical Integrity

**Severity:**
Medium 🟡

---

### 4. Inconsistent survey deletion behavior across roles

**Observed behavior:**

* Deleted surveys disappear for Instructor
* Student completed history still displays deleted surveys

**Impact:**
Causes divergence between Instructor and Student lifecycle states.

**Type:**
Workflow Consistency

**Severity:**
Medium 🟡

---

### 5. Missing instructor preview simulation

**Observed behavior:**

* Instructor preview mode does not render surveys as a Student view

**Impact:**
Limits validation of final survey experience before publication.

**Type:**
UX / Missing Feature

**Severity:**
Low 🟢

## Conclusion

The system provides a functional end-to-end workflow for survey management, response collection, and result monitoring.

Core survey operations behave correctly, including:

* survey creation
* question management
* response submission
* role separation

However, several important inconsistencies remain regarding:

* validation enforcement
* incomplete response persistence
* historical data consistency
* preview behavior

From a QA perspective, the platform is:

* operational at workflow level
* but partially inconsistent during advanced survey lifecycle scenarios
